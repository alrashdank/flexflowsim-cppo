**Biographical note**

Khaled R. Alrashdan is a faculty member in the Department of Manufacturing
Engineering Technology, College of Technological Studies, at the Public
Authority for Applied Education and Training in Kuwait. His research concerns
the modelling and control of manufacturing systems, with a focus on
discrete-event simulation of multi-stage production lines, dispatching and
routing policies, and the empirical evaluation of machine-learning methods
applied to production control. His recent work benchmarks reinforcement learning
against tuned dispatching rules in flow shops subject to disruption, with an
emphasis on reproducible protocols and open simulation software.
