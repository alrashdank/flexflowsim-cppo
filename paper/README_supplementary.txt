Supplementary material for "Why Constrained Reinforcement Learning Appeared to Fail on Flow-Shop
Routing: Two Artefacts in Training and Evaluation, and What Their Correction Reveals"
(anonymised, under double-blind review at TMLR)

S1_original_submission.pdf  The earlier manuscript referred to throughout as "the original
                            submission" (Author, 2026a), anonymised. Its Tables 3 and 5 are the
                            figures reproduced to the cent in Section 6.3 of the present paper.
S2_code_and_results.zip     Anonymised snapshot of the companion code (Author, 2026b) with the
                            archived checkpoints of the original submission, all protocol documents,
                            per-seed summaries, lambda histories, validation caches, per-episode
                            test records and the selected checkpoints of every run reported. See
                            README_SUPPLEMENT.md inside for how each table is regenerated.
